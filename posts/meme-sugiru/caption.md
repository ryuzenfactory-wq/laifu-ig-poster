Stick すぎ on the end and it means too much of that thing.

高い → 高すぎ, way too expensive
辛い → 辛すぎ, way too spicy
食べる → 食べすぎ, ate way too much

Then いい breaks the pattern. It becomes よすぎ, not いいすぎ, because いい turns into よ before
almost every ending. いいすぎ (言い過ぎ) is a real word, and it means you said too much.

Two different sentences. Learn that one on purpose.
