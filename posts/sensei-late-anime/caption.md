Excuses for being late in Japanese 🏃

電車が遅れました。(densha ga okuremashita) My train was late.
家のハムスターが脱走しました。(ie no hamusutā ga dassō shimashita) My hamster escaped.
UFOに連れ去られていました。(yūfō ni tsuresararete imashita) I was abducted by a UFO.

The first one actually works. Trains here are so punctual that a delay counts as a real excuse, and rail companies even issue delay certificates (遅延証明書) you can show at school or work. The other two? Use at your own risk 😂
