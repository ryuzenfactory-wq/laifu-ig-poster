The first 60 seconds inside a Japanese restaurant, handled 👉

何名様ですか (nan-mei sama desu ka) is the first thing you'll hear. Then it's party size, reservation or not, how long the wait is, and where you'd like to sit.

Six lines and you're seated. That's the part that trips people up, and it's over in a minute.
