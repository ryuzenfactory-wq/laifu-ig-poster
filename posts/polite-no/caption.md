In Japanese, a flat 'no' usually gets softened. Here's what you'll hear instead 👉

ちょっと… (chotto...), 考えておきます (kangaete okimasu), また今度 (mata kondo). Depending on tone and context, these usually mean no. Learn to hear it, and to say it.
