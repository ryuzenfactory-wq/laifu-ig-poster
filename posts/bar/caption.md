Your first drink at a Japanese bar 👉

とりあえずビール (toriaezu biiru) is the standard opener. Then お通し (otoushi) arrives, a small dish you didn't order and will be charged for. That's normal, it's the cover.

お通しって何ですか (otoushi tte nan desu ka) is a fair question to ask, and nobody minds you asking.
