だいじょうぶです might be the most useful word in Japanese, and it means both yes and no.

Someone offers you a bag: it means no thanks.
Someone asks if you're hurt: it means I'm fine.

The reply never changes. The question decides. So if you're being offered something,
assume it's a no.
