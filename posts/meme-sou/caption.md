Stick そう on an adjective and it becomes "looks like".

おいしい → おいしそう, looks tasty
たのしい → たのしそう, looks fun
むずかしい → むずかしそう, looks hard

Then かわいい → かわいそう, which is not "looks cute". It means "poor thing".
Say that about someone's outfit and you have a situation.
