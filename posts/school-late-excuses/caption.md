Every Japanese teacher has heard 寝坊しました a hundred times. What they have not heard is a student calmly explaining that a UFO was involved.

The joke works because the grammar never changes. 〜ました and 〜ていました the whole way down, so the ridiculous excuses drill the exact same pattern as the real ones. Learn the funny one and you have learned the useful one.

7 excuses, ranked from believable to please-do-not 👉

寝坊しました。Nebō shimashita. = I overslept.
電車が遅れました。Densha ga okuremashita. = My train was late.
家のハムスターが脱走しました。Ie no hamusutā ga dassō shimashita. = My hamster escaped.
ヤンキーに絡まれていました。Yankī ni karamarete imashita. = Some delinquents were hassling me.
金縛りに遭って動けませんでした。Kanashibari ni atte ugokemasen deshita. = I had sleep paralysis and couldn't move.
UFOに連れ去られていました。Yūfō ni tsuresararete imashita. = I was abducted by a UFO.
気をつけます。Ki o tsukemasu. = I'll be careful.

That last one is the one to actually memorise. Whatever excuse you go with, it is how you close the conversation.
