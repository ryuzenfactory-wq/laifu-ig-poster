雷 is thunder.

Say it かみなり and you sound like a person.
Say it らい and you're inside a compound word like 雷雨.
Say it いかずち and you sound like you're narrating a battle scene.

One character, three moods. This is the part nobody warns you about before you start.

Start with かみなり. That's the one you'll actually hear. Link in bio.
