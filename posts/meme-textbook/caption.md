The textbook version isn't wrong. It's just not what anyone says.

わたしはビールがのみたいです。
Watashi wa biiru ga nomitai desu.

Nobody announces who they are before ordering a drink. Japanese drops the subject
the moment it's obvious, and at an izakaya it's always obvious.

ビールください。
Biiru kudasai.

That's the whole sentence. Link in bio.
