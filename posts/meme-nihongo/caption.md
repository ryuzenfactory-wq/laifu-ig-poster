わたしはにほんごがすこしはなせます。

Grammatically perfect. Also about four words longer than anyone speaks.

にほんご、ちょっとだけ。

Japanese plays skills down by default, and ちょっとだけ is the standard way to do it. Say the
long version and people hear a textbook. Say the short one and they just start talking to you.
