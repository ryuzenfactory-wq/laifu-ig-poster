おてあらいはどこにありますか。

That is the sentence textbooks give you for finding a toilet. It is correct, and nobody says it.

トイレ、どこですか？

Still polite, half the length, and the word everyone actually uses. おてあらい is the formal
one, saved for places that care about being formal.
