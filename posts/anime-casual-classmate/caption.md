What you say to the person sitting next to you is much shorter than what the textbook teaches you to say.

今どこ？Ima doko? = Where are we? (as in, what page)
見せて。Misete. = Let me see.
全然わかんない。Zenzen wakannai. = I'm completely lost.

Two words, one word, three words. The polite forms you learned are for the teacher. These are for your classmate, and using the wrong register in either direction is what actually sounds strange.

見せて is a bare 〜て request, which only works between friends. わかんない is how わからない comes out of a real mouth. And 今どこ has no verb at all, because nobody needs one.
