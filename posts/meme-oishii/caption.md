All four of these mean the food is good.

うまい, casual, mates only
おいしい, safe absolutely anywhere
おいしいです, polite, use it with staff
おいしゅうございます, technically correct, roughly 1950s

The gap that actually matters is うまい to おいしいです. Pick wrong and you either sound
rude or sound like you're reading off a card.
