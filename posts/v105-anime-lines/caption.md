Anime teaches you the words. It does not teach you which room they belong in, and that is the part that gets people in trouble.

待って is completely safe. You can say it to anyone, and adding kudasai makes it polite enough for strangers. ふざけるな is not the same kind of line at all. It is genuinely angry, and it lands that way even when you meant it as a joke.

So here are 5 anime lines with the note that actually matters 👉

待って！ Matte! = Wait!
覚悟しろ Kakugo shiro = Get ready for this
ふざけるな Fuzakeru na = Don't screw with me
助けて！ Tasukete! = Help!
任せろ Makasero = Leave it to me

助けて is the one worth memorising for real. It is not anime language, it is what you shout if something actually goes wrong.
