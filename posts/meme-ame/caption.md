雨 is rain. 飴 is candy.

Both are あめ. Both are two syllables. The only thing separating them is pitch, which is the
part almost nobody teaches you.

あめの日に、あめを買った。 That sentence is real, and yes, it has both.

Context carries most of it. The pitch is the last five percent.
