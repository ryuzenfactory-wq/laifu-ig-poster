The most useful sentence in any Japanese classroom is not an answer. It is asking someone to say it again.

もう一度お願いします。Mō ichido onegaishimasu. = One more time, please.
もう少しゆっくりお願いします。Mō sukoshi yukkuri onegaishimasu. = A little slower, please.
どういう意味ですか。Dō iu imi desu ka. = What does that mean?

The second one does more work than the first. A repeat at the same speed rarely helps, so asking for slower is the better move. And the third is for a word you heard perfectly clearly and still do not know, which happens constantly.

Say these early and say them often. Nobody thinks less of you for it, and the alternative is nodding through a whole lesson you did not follow.
