Nobody warns you that the hard part of a Japanese classroom is not speaking. It is the three seconds after the teacher says something and the whole room starts moving.

教科書を開いてください。Kyōkasho o hiraite kudasai. = Open your textbook.
プリントを後ろに回してください。Purinto o ushiro ni mawashite kudasai. = Take one handout and pass the rest behind you.
二人組になってください。Futari-gumi ni natte kudasai. = Get into pairs.

You never have to say any of these. You just have to catch them, and the whole lesson stops being stressful. プリント is a handout, 一枚 is the counter for flat things, and 回す is to send something round the room.

The third one is the moment to turn to the person beside you, before you are the only one sitting alone.
