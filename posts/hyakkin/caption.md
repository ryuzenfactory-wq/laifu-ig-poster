The 100 yen shop is the best souvenir shop in Japan 👉

全部100円ですか (zenbu hyaku-en desu ka) is worth asking, because not everything is. And the price on the shelf is often 税抜き (zeinuki), before tax.

風呂敷 (furoshiki), お弁当箱 (obentou-bako), 折り紙 (origami). People fly home with suitcases of this stuff.
