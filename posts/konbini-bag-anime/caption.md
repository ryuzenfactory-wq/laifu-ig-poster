At a konbini in Japan 🏪

袋いりますか？ (fukuro irimasu ka?) Do you need a bag?
大丈夫です (daijōbu desu) I'm good

Plastic bags cost a few yen in Japan, so the cashier will usually ask. Don't need one? 大丈夫です is all you have to say. Here it means "No thanks, I'm good."
