Ordering coffee in Japan is three questions 👉

ホットですか、アイスですか (hotto desu ka, aisu desu ka). サイズはどうしますか (saizu wa dou shimasu ka). And whether you're staying or taking it away.

Answer those three and you're done. ここで食べます (koko de tabemasu) means you're staying.
