Japanese has a word for every way you can overdo it, and your friends always have a reply ready.

食っちゃ寝 kuccha-ne is eat, sleep, repeat. Admit you spent the whole break doing it and the answer is 最高の休みじゃん, "sounds like the perfect break."

別腹 betsubara is the separate stomach that dessert goes into. When a friend asks if you're really still eating, デザートは別腹だよ is your answer, and the reply you get is その胃袋うらやましい, "I envy that stomach."

元気の前借り genki no maegari is borrowing energy from tomorrow. Say it over another energy drink and you get a warning back: あとで反動くるよ, "you'll crash later."
