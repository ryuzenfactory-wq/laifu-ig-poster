Standing at a shrine wondering what to do with your hands? 👉

二礼二拍手一礼 (nirei nihakushu ichirei). Two bows, two claps, one bow. Rinse at the 手水舎 (temizuya) first, hat off, coin in, then the bows.

Draw an おみくじ (omikuji) after. If it's 凶 (kyou), you tie it to the rack and leave it there. If it's 大吉 (daikichi), you keep it.
