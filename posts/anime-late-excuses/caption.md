The excuse is ridiculous. The grammar is not.

ユーフォーに連れ去られていました is something your teacher has heard before, and they were not impressed then either. But it runs on the same pattern as the excuse you will actually need one day. 〜ていました, all the way down. Learn the silly one and the useful one comes free.

電車が遅れました。Densha ga okuremashita. = My train was late. This one gets believed here, because it is usually true.

自転車がパンクしました。Jitensha ga panku shimashita. = I got a flat tyre. パンク is from puncture, nothing to do with the music.

UFOに連れ去られていました。Yūfō ni tsuresararete imashita. = I was abducted by a UFO. Deploy at your own risk.

Whatever you go with, close it with 気をつけます。Ki o tsukemasu. = I'll be careful. That is the line that ends the conversation, and it works for every telling-off you will ever get.
