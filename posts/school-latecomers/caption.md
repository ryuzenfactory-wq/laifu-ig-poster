Part 1 was the excuse you give your teacher. This is the one you give the person sitting next to you, and it is a completely different language.

すみません、遅れました becomes また遅刻？. Same situation, same morning, and every polite ending gets filed off. That switch is the thing Japanese classrooms actually demand of you, because the teacher and your friend are in the same room.

Watch where this one ends up 👉

また遅刻？ Mata chikoku? = Late again?
ぜんぜん信じてない。Zenzen shinjitenai. = I don't believe you for a second.
UFOに連れ去られてた。Yūfō ni tsuresararete ta. = I got abducted by a UFO.

The person calling out the excuse had the same excuse. じゃあ、おあいこだね。
