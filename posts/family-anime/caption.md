Family in Japanese 🇯🇵

お父さん (otōsan) dad
お母さん (okāsan) mom
お兄さん (onīsan) big brother
お姉さん (onēsan) big sister
弟 (otōto) little brother
妹 (imōto) little sister
おじいさん (ojīsan) grandpa
おばあさん (obāsan) grandma

When you talk about your own family to someone else, people often use plainer words: 父 (chichi) for your dad and 母 (haha) for your mom.
