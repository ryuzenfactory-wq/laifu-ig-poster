Buying おみやげ (omiyage) is its own skill 👉

日持ちしますか (himochi shimasu ka) asks if it keeps. 個包装になってますか (kobousou ni natte masu ka) asks if it's wrapped individually, which is what makes it work for an office.

That second one matters more than it sounds. Omiyage is usually something you hand out one by one.
