Four ways to apologize, smallest to largest.

ごめん, friends only
ごめんなさい, everyday
すみません, polite, works almost anywhere
申し訳ありません, you broke something

The one that matters is ごめん to すみません. Use ごめん with a stranger and it lands too light.
Use 申し訳ありません for a bumped shoulder and everyone gets uncomfortable.

すみません is the safe default. Link in bio.
