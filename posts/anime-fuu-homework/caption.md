Three things you will need in a Japanese classroom, and not one of them is an excuse.

宿題はいつまでですか。Shukudai wa itsu made desu ka. = When is the homework due?
教科書を忘れました。Kyōkasho o wasuremashita. = I forgot my textbook.
プリント、もう一枚もらえますか。Purinto, mō ichimai moraemasu ka. = Could I get another handout?

いつまで asks for the deadline, not the day it was set. 忘れました covers both forgetting a thing and leaving it behind, so it works whether the textbook is at home or on your desk.

None of these are a big deal here. Say the second one before you are asked, and it stops being a problem. Saying nothing is what makes it one.
