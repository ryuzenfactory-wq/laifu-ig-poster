生 might be the hardest working kanji in Japanese.

なま, raw. 生ビール is nama biiru, draft beer.
せい, inside compound words. 学生 is gakusei, student.
い(きる), to live. 生きる, same character.

And that is only the start of the list.

Learn なま first. It is the one standing between you and a cold beer.
